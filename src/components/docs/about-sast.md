# Society for Astrophysics and Space Technology (SAST)

At **SAST**, we build community projects, documentation, and learning resources together. We are a dynamic community of space enthusiasts, researchers, and innovators dedicated to advancing our understanding of the cosmos and developing cutting-edge space technologies.

## Our Mission
- Foster innovation and collaboration in space technology
- Build accessible projects and resources for students
- Create a welcoming path for newcomers into open source
- Promote space education and public outreach
- Grow a vibrant community around astrophysics and space technology

## What We Do

### Open Source Projects
- **Satellite Tracking System**: Real-time monitoring of satellites and space debris
- **Space Weather Dashboard**: Analysis and visualization of space weather data
- **Astronomical Tools**: Open-source software for space data analysis
- **Educational Resources**: Documentation and learning materials for space enthusiasts

### Community Activities
- Regular technical workshops and meetups
- Mentorship programs for newcomers
- Collaborative coding sessions
- Space technology discussions and talks

## How to Get Started

### Newcomer Path
1. Join our community and introduce yourself in **#introductions**
2. Pick a `good first issue` in any repository
3. Open your first PR—our mentors will guide you
4. Participate in community discussions and events

### Ways to Contribute
- Code contributions to our projects
- Documentation improvements
- Bug reports and feature suggestions
- Community support and mentoring
- Content creation and tutorials
